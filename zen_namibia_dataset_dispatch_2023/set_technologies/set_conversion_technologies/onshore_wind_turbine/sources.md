onshore_wind_turbine
====================

capacity_existing
-----------------

Step 1
Existing 2023 wind fleet capacity from renewable_plants.csv plus the Omburu PV row in capacities_<reference_year>.csv where applicable.

**Citation**

Renewable Ninja, ZEN Namibia (2023). Namibia renewable plant capacities and capacity factors. Renewable Ninja plant.

capacity_limit
--------------

Step 1
Total installed 2023 wind capacity from renewable_plants.csv plus the Omburu PV row in capacities_<reference_year>.csv where applicable.

**Citation**

Renewable Ninja, ZEN Namibia (2023). Namibia renewable plant capacities and capacity factors. Renewable Ninja plant.

max_load
--------

Step 1
Hourly 2023 Renewables.ninja power profiles for the wind plants, normalized by each profile's simulated capacity and weighted by installed capacity.

**Citation**

Renewable Ninja, ZEN Namibia (2023). Namibia renewable plant capacities and capacity factors. Renewable Ninja plant.
